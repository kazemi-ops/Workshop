Topics

The workshop welcomes submissions on a broad range of topics related to entrepreneurship and AI. Areas of interest include, but are not limited to:

  • Implications of AI for the organization of firms and markets
  • Entrepreneurship and innovation
  • Technology adoption, and how AI and humans cooperate
  • Labor markets, re- and upskilling
  • Organizational learning
  • Entrepreneurial selection and mentorship
  • Strategic management decisions under uncertainty
  • Governance and risk management
  • Comparisons with past technology revolutions
